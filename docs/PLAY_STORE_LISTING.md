## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Advottic helps you walk into court prepared. Whether you have a hearing tomorrow
or you're still figuring out what happened, this is a calm place to gather the
evidence, write down the dates, and end up with one clean file you can share with
a lawyer in five minutes.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- Build a case file. Capture the parties, jurisdiction, posture, and the story
  in your own words. Edit any time.
- Add exhibits. Photos of citations, screenshots of texts, PDFs of forms,
  voice memos - drop them in and Advottic auto-numbers them, captures the date,
  and tags the source.
- Get a structured review. Advottic Review surfaces possible issues, evidence
  gaps, and questions worth asking your attorney. Plain language, jurisdiction-
  aware, never a substitute for a licensed lawyer.
- Track hearings. See your next court date on the dashboard, get countdown
  reminders, and pull a one-page summary the morning of.
- Export a packet. One PDF with the case summary, all numbered exhibits, and
  the questions list - the file your attorney can skim in the elevator.
- Ask Bella. The in-app assistant explains legal terms in plain English and
  helps you find what you're looking for inside your own case file.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

- Encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256).
- Your case content is yours. We do not sell it, train outside models on it,
  or share it with marketing partners.
- Export your full case archive (JSON + files) any time. Delete your account
  in one tap from Profile - everything goes, no support ticket required.

WHAT ADVOTTIC IS, AND ISN'T

Advottic is a case-organization and preparation tool. It is NOT a law firm,
does NOT provide legal advice, and does NOT create an attorney-client
relationship. AI-generated content can be incomplete, outdated, or wrong -
always consult a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction before acting. If
you face possible incarceration, ask the court for a public defender at your
first appearance. That's a free constitutional right.

WHO IT'S FOR

- Self-represented people who want to show up organized.
- People with an attorney who want to hand over a clean packet instead of a
  stack of screenshots.
- Attorneys, paralegals, and law-firm staff who want a calm intake surface
  per matter (Counsel mode).

PRICING

Free 7-day trial. After that, plans start at the Basic tier; Standard adds
unlimited Advottic Review. No card required to start. Cancel any time from
Profile.

CONTACT

Questions, feedback, or stuck on something - contact@advottic.com. We read
every message.
```
*~2,160 chars; well under the 4000 limit. Includes the keywords Play search
ranks on (case, evidence, hearing, attorney, court, organize, packet),
disclaims aggressively (matches /about + /privacy on the website), and
discloses the trial + paid tier so reviewers don't flag it later.*

## Required graphic assets

Play Console wants these files BEFORE you can submit. Sizes are mandatory.

| Asset | Size | Source |
|---|---|---|
| App icon | 512 x 512 px (PNG, 32-bit, alpha-OK) | derived from `assets/icon-only.png` (already in repo) |
| Feature graphic | 1024 x 500 px (PNG, no transparency) | needs custom artwork - see note below |
| Phone screenshots | min 2, max 8; 16:9 or 9:16; 320-3840 px shortest side | take from real device or emulator |
| 7-inch tablet screenshots | optional but boosts ranking; 1024 x 600+ | optional |
| 10-inch tablet screenshots | optional; 1280 x 800+ | optional |

The feature graphic is the wide banner Play shows above the install button. Two
options:
1. **Quick:** wordmark on the forest-green brand color with one-line tagline ("Walk into court prepared"). Can be done in 10 minutes in Figma / Canva.
2. **Considered:** screenshot of the case dashboard composited into a phone frame on the brand background. Higher conversion in our testing.
