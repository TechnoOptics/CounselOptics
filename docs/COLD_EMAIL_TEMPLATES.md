# Cold email templates

The compounding flywheel (SEO + content) takes 3-6 months to pay rent.
This document is the "what pays rent in the meantime" play.

Each template targets a specific audience with a specific call-to-action.
Templates that have produced replies in legal-tech outbound (per Lawyerist
benchmarks): personalized first sentence, single-question CTA, under 120
words. Long emails get archived; short asks get answered.

**Personalize the first sentence.** Every template has a `[PERSONALIZE]`
slot. Skip it and your reply rate falls 60-80%. Spend 90 seconds reading
the recipient's bio / firm site / recent post and write one specific line
about them.

**Send from a real human address.** `abel@advottic.com` (or your real
name @ advottic.com), not `hello@` or `support@`. Spam filters down-rank
generic role addresses; humans down-rank them harder.

**Track replies, not opens.** Open-tracking pixels get filtered into
spam. Replies are the only metric that matters.

## Operating cadence

- **Monday morning**: send 15 personalized cold emails (60-90 minutes
  of work). Personalize each one - the formula is "their thing in 1
  sentence" + "your thing in 2 sentences" + "ask in 1 sentence".
- **Wednesday**: send 5 follow-ups to people who didn't reply. Format:
  reply to the original thread, add one new sentence ("re-upping in
  case this got buried - I'd really value 15 minutes if you can spare
  it"), don't re-pitch.
- **Friday afternoon**: log replies in a simple spreadsheet (name, org,
  date, status, next action). Replies that don't convert are still
  warm: tag them and re-touch in 90 days.

**Key metric**: replies, not opens. Track in a spreadsheet:
`name | org | template_used | sent | replied | next_action`. Aim for
8-12% reply rate from cold; 30-50% from warm intros.
