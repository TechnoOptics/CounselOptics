```
I'm Abel Muchai, founder of Techno Optics LLC, the company behind Advottic.

Most people facing a legal problem aren't trying to "win the internet." They're
trying to keep track of what happened, understand the rules that apply to them,
and walk into a courtroom or an attorney's office without feeling lost. The
tools that exist are either expensive software built for firms, or a folder of
screenshots and a legal pad. We built Advottic for the gap in between.

For individuals, Advottic is a calm place to:
- Organize a matter in one timeline, with exhibits labeled and dated.
- Draft a clear demand letter from a guided template.
- Ask Bella, the in-app assistant, plain-English questions about process.
- Run "Advottic Review" over your own documents to spot gaps before a hearing.
- Export a case packet a reader can absorb in about five minutes.
- Use Safe Witness, an optional personal-safety check-in with location sharing
  from a phone or a Wear OS watch, for people who feel unsafe.

For law firms, Advottic Counsel runs the practice in one place: case
management, time and billing, IOLTA trust accounting, e-signature, and an AI
assistant that helps with the routine parts (conflict checks, time entries,
first-draft documents) under the attorney's supervision.

Free, no signup required:
- Templates library (demand letter, NDA, cease and desist, lease termination,
  security-deposit demand): https://advottic.com/templates
- Statute-of-limitations checker, 50 states + DC, 9 claim categories:
  https://advottic.com/tools/statute-of-limitations
- The underlying data as open CC BY 4.0 JSON: https://advottic.com/open-data
- An embeddable version of the checker for legal-aid sites:
  https://advottic.com/tools/statute-of-limitations/embed

Stack: Next.js 14, Supabase, Stripe, Twilio, Wear OS (Kotlin Compose),
pdf-lib + PDFKit for packet export. Capacitor shells for iOS/Android.

This is information and organization, not legal advice. I'd genuinely value
feedback on the consumer experience and on the SOL data. Happy to talk shop
on any of the stack.
```
