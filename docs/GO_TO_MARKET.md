# Go-to-market: Advottic, 30 / 60 / 90

The honest plan. SEO is a 3-6 month game; this document is built
around the reality that you cannot Google-Ads your way out of a
$0/mo run rate, and you cannot SEO your way to revenue this week.

So we run two motions in parallel:

1. **Compounding flywheel** (SEO + content + product-led growth) -
   builds slowly, but every dollar invested pays back forever.
2. **Direct outbound** (cold email + AppSumo + bar-association
   outreach) - cash this week, dies the moment we stop touching it.

Both are required. Neither is enough alone.

## Day 1-30: foundation that stops bleeding

### Distribution surfaces (already shipped)

- `/resources` hub with 8 cornerstone SEO articles, each targeting
  a real high-intent keyword cluster.
- `/press`, `/affiliate`, `/developers` marketing pages - signals
  to Google that this is a real, multi-faceted company, not a
  landing-page-on-a-domain.
- Schema.org JSON-LD on every key surface (Organization, WebSite,
  SoftwareApplication, FAQPage, Service, BreadcrumbList, Article).
  Eligible for rich results within ~2 weeks of indexing.
- Sitemap auto-includes every published article. Robots blocks
  authenticated routes from leaking into search.

### What to do this week

1. **Submit the sitemap** to:
   - Google Search Console (`google.com/webmasters` -> Sitemaps).
   - Bing Webmaster Tools (`bing.com/webmasters`).
   - IndexNow (`indexnow.org`) - one-shot ping that Bing + Yandex
     accept, faster crawl than waiting for organic discovery.
2. **Verify ownership** of:
   - Google Business Profile (already filed; postcard pending).
   - Search Console for `advottic.com` and `www.advottic.com`.
   - Bing Webmaster Tools.
3. **Write 4 more articles** (target: 12 cornerstone pieces by
   day 30). Suggested keywords (all >1k monthly searches, low to
   medium difficulty):
   - "small claims court process"
   - "what is an attorney engagement letter"
   - "how to fire your lawyer"
   - "what does an estate planning attorney do"
4. **Set up email capture** on the homepage and `/resources`. A
   simple "get the next guide in your inbox" form. Use the
   existing newsletter list; sync to Stripe via the email field.
5. **Submit Advottic to**:
   - Product Hunt (Tuesday morning launch; coordinate with at
     least 5 hunters who will upvote in the first hour).
   - AppSumo (lifetime-deal listing - $79 / $129 tiers; AppSumo
     takes 70%, but the volume + reviews are worth it).
   - G2 Crowd (legal practice management category).
   - Capterra / GetApp (Gartner-owned; legal directory category).

### What direct outbound looks like

**Target list (build this week, send next week):**

- **Bar associations**: every state bar's small-firm or solo-firm
  practice management committee. There are 50 state bars + DC; each
  has a small-firm liaison. Email:
  > "We built Advottic Counsel as the first practice-management
  > tool with a competent AI agent built in. $59/user/mo, 14-day
  > free trial, no card. Would the small-firm committee be open to
  > a 20-minute walkthrough?"
- **Legal-tech newsletters**: Above the Law, LawSites (Bob
  Ambrogi - has a public review queue), Artificial Lawyer, Robert
  Ambrogi's "LawNext" podcast. Each one is a single email, but a
  positive mention compounds for years.
- **Law-school career services**: most career-services offices
  send a weekly digest to graduating students. A short intro about
  "free Bella access for 1L through 3L" gets to thousands of
  future-attorneys for the cost of one email.
- **Pro-se support orgs**: NCRA (National Coalition for a Civil
  Right to Counsel), legal-aid societies. Pitch: free Personal Pro
  for legal-aid clients, sponsored by Advottic.

### Day 30 success metric

- 12 published articles, each with at least 3 internal links from
  other articles. Sitemap fully indexed. 50 cold emails sent. 5
  positive replies. 1 newsletter mention secured. **100 free
  signups, 5 paid conversions.** Revenue: ~$200/mo MRR.

## Day 31-60: density + paid-channel testing

### What changes at day 31

You now have 30 days of data. You know:

- Which articles are getting impressions (from Search Console).
- Which signups are converting to paid (from Stripe).
- Which cold-outbound channels actually replied.

### Action items

1. **Double down on the article archetypes that won.** If "best
   legal AI 2026" got the most impressions, write a second piece
   targeting "legal AI for small firms" and a third on "is legal
   AI safe to use in client work". One winning article unlocks a
   cluster.

2. **Start paid acquisition - small.** $500/week test on:
   - Google Ads on **non-brand long-tail**. Avoid bidding on
     "lawyer" or "legal AI" - too expensive. Bid on:
     - `iolta accounting software` ($3-$8 CPC, low competition)
     - `e signature for law firms` ($2-$5 CPC)
     - `practice management for solo lawyers` ($4-$10 CPC)
   - Facebook / Instagram on **Personal Pro** to lookalike audiences
     of your existing free-tier users. Creative: short video
     showing Bella drafting a demand letter in 90 seconds.

3. **Affiliate program activation.** The `/affiliate` page is up;
   now manually outreach to:
   - 20 legal-tech YouTubers (Lawyer Up, Attorney Tom, LegalEagle).
     Most charge $2-5k for a 60-second integrated mention; for
     legal-tech creators, a 30% recurring affiliate is genuinely
     better economics than a flat fee.
   - 10 legal-tech newsletters (Lawyerist, Above the Law, LawNext).
     Most have an "industry partners" tier - $500-$2000/mo for
     placement; affiliate-sponsored placement is much cheaper.

4. **Backlink campaign.** This is the slowest, highest-leverage
   channel. Goal: 20 high-DA backlinks by day 60.
   - Submit guest posts to: Lawyerist, Attorney at Work, Above
     the Law, Above the Law's "Small Law" section, Robert
     Ambrogi's LawSites.
   - Earn natural backlinks: write the 3 best articles on a
     specific topic (e.g. "IOLTA accounting software comparison")
     and email every legal-tech roundup writer with the link.
   - HARO (Help a Reporter Out): set up 3 daily alerts for
     "legal AI", "legal tech", "small law firm software"; reply
     to every relevant query with a quote attributed to the
     founder. Each placement = one DA-70+ backlink.

5. **Bar-association outreach part 2: speaking slots.** Most
   state bars have an annual conference; the AI / legal-tech
   track is the easiest to crack into. Pitch a 30-minute talk on
   "How small firms can adopt AI without bar-rule violations"
   (the talk should be 5% Advottic, 95% genuinely useful framework).

### Day 60 success metric

- 20 published articles, 20 high-DA backlinks, 3 newsletter
  mentions, 1 podcast appearance. Paid CAC < $80. **500 free
  signups cumulatively, 30 paid conversions.** Revenue: ~$1,500/mo
  MRR. Press: at least one mention in a top-3 legal-tech outlet.

## Day 61-90: leveraged growth

By day 60 you should be able to clearly see:

- **Which keyword clusters are winning** (Search Console - sort
  by impressions, then click-through-rate).
- **Which paid channels payback in under 6 months** (Stripe LTV
  divided by ad spend).
- **Which firms have signed and are using the product** (the
  testimonial goldmine).

### Action items

1. **Publish 4 more articles per month.** Hit 30 articles total by
   day 90. The article-count flywheel: each article that ranks
   sends signals to the others (internal links, dwell time, return
   visits), so the 30th article ranks faster than the 5th.

2. **Build customer stories.** Email every paying firm with the
   pitch: "we'll write a case study highlighting how you use
   Advottic, gives you a backlink + featured logo on our site,
   takes 30 minutes of your time." Aim for 5 case studies by day
   90; each one is a long-tail SEO asset *and* a sales asset.

3. **Launch a webinar series.** Monthly, 30 minutes, free. Topics:
   - "Running an IOLTA-compliant trust account with zero spreadsheets"
   - "What every small firm should know about UETA and e-sign"
   - "How to use legal AI without violating client confidentiality"
   Each webinar = a recorded asset that doubles as SEO content +
   a lead-gen form.

4. **Annual prepay push.** Advottic offers 20% off annual prepay,
   which means a $19/mo Personal Pro becomes $182/yr (effective
   $15.17/mo). For firms, the math is bigger: $59/user/mo Solo
   becomes $566/user/yr. Sales motion: every active monthly
   subscriber gets a "switch to annual, pocket 20%, plus
   double Bella tokens" offer at the 3-month mark.

5. **Referral program.** "Refer a friend, you both get $20
   credit." Wire it into the dashboard at `/welcome` and `/invite`.
   For Counsel firms: refer another firm, both get one month
   free.

### Day 90 success metric

- 30 articles, 50 backlinks, 5 case studies. Organic search is
  now bringing 1,000+ visits/month. Paid CAC < $60. **2,000 free
  signups cumulatively, 150 paid conversions.** Revenue:
  ~$8,000/mo MRR. Press: 3 newsletter mentions, 1 podcast appearance,
  1 conference talk booked.

## Honest bottom line

In the absolute best case, all of the above gets Advottic to
$8-12k/mo MRR by day 90. That is not a "feed your family" outcome
on its own - it is the foundation on which a "feed your family"
business is built.

The compounding curve is slow at first, vertical at the end. The
ratio is roughly: months 1-3 you get 5% of total signups, months
4-12 you get 25%, months 13-24 you get 70%. The same article
written today is worth 10-30x more in 18 months than in 3 months,
because Google trusts a domain that has been publishing
consistently for 18 months and trusts a domain that just launched
~zero.

**What pays the bills in the meantime** is direct outbound. Cold
emails to bar associations, cold emails to law-firm partners,
cold emails to legal-aid societies. One $99/user/mo Small Firm
deal with 5 seats is $495/mo. One Enterprise deal at 100 seats
is $14,900/mo. **Two Enterprise deals pay rent for the year.**

So while the SEO flywheel spins up, every spare hour goes into
building the outbound list and personalizing the cold emails.
The combination is what wins.

## What is intentionally NOT in this plan

- **Free-trial extensions / coupon spam.** Burns the brand for
  immaterial revenue. The 14-day trial converts at 8-15% in
  legal-tech; squeezing it to 17% via discount spam is not worth
  the long-term LTV damage.
- **Press releases via PR Newswire.** $400-$800 per release for
  zero meaningful pickup. Direct journalist outreach is free and
  10x more effective.
- **Influencer marketing on TikTok / Instagram (consumer-side).**
  Legal services have a long consideration cycle; impulse signups
  off a 30-second video do not convert. Save the budget for
  legal-tech YouTubers (educational format = matched intent).
- **Conferences in the first 90 days.** ABA Tech Show, Clio Con,
  ILTACON. These are $5-$10k of booth + travel + swag, and the
  ROI requires the product to already have brand recognition. By
  month 6 they make sense.

## Operating cadence

- **Weekly**: 1 new article, 1 update to an existing article, 20
  cold emails sent, Search Console review (top-10 movers + losers).
- **Bi-weekly**: paid-ads creative refresh, affiliate-payout
  review, customer-story outreach.
- **Monthly**: full GTM retro - what worked, what to cut, where
  to double down. Update this document.
